# Qullamaggie Breakout

A continuation setup popularized by Kristjan Kullamägi (a.k.a. "Qullamaggie"). After a strong directional leg, the stock consolidates in a tight range, and the breakout from the consolidation kicks off a second leg.

## Why It Works

The initial leg (typically +30% in 30–90 days) marks the stock as an emerging leader. The tight consolidation that follows is profit-taking from early holders being absorbed by a new wave of institutional buyers. The second leg is the genuine extension, often a multiple of the first leg's length.

> "I look for stocks that just had a big move — 30%, 50%, 100% in 1 to 3 months — and then go sideways. That's where the money is." — Kristjan Kullamägi

## Entry Rules

1. Prior directional leg of 30%+ over 30–90 calendar days (the "ADR move").
2. Tight consolidation 5–15 bars in a range less than ~10% of the leg height.
3. Average daily range (ADR%) compressing through the consolidation (volatility drying up).
4. Price stays above the rising EMA10 or EMA20 during the base.
5. RS rank above 90.
6. Breakout above the consolidation high on a volume surge (1.5x+ the 50-day average).

## Exit Rules

- **Stop Loss:** Below the consolidation low or 1.5x ATR below entry, whichever is tighter.
- **Target 1:** 2.0x ATR above entry — scale out 50%.
- **Target 2:** Trail remaining position with a moving stop (EMA10 break or 1.5x ATR from highs).
- **Maximum hold:** 25 trading days. If the second leg hasn't materialized by then, the setup is broken.

## Ideal Market Conditions

Bull market only. The setup depends on broad risk-on momentum. In ranging or choppy regimes the second leg fails to materialize and the consolidation simply breaks down. ADR compression during the base is the load-bearing diagnostic — without it, the "consolidation" is just a stalled stock, not a coiled spring.

## Source Attribution

The framework is Kristjan Kullamägi's, taught extensively in his public materials and recorded interviews. Underlying mechanics overlap with O'Neil-school continuation patterns but the specific entry rules (ADR compression, prior-leg magnitude, RS gate) are Kullamägi's contribution.
