# Trend Template Fresh Pass

Mark Minervini's Trend Template is the eight-point checklist a stock must clear to qualify as a Stage 2 momentum long. The Fresh Pass variant fires on the FIRST day a stock newly satisfies the template after a period of failing it — capturing the moment the name joins the leadership cohort.

## Why the Fresh-Pass Variant

Stocks already deep into the Trend Template have run a significant distance from their breakout. Entries this far into the move have lower expected R-multiples and higher drawdown risk. The "fresh pass" — the first day in N days (typically 20) where every template condition is satisfied — is the cleanest version of the entry: it's when the stock joins the leadership cohort, not after it has already led for weeks.

## The Trend Template Conditions

A stock satisfies Minervini's Trend Template when ALL of the following are true:

1. Price above SMA150 AND price above SMA200.
2. SMA150 above SMA200.
3. SMA200 sloped upward for at least 1 month (preferably 4–5 months).
4. SMA50 above SMA150 AND SMA50 above SMA200.
5. Price above SMA50.
6. Price at least 30% above its 52-week low.
7. Price within 25% of its 52-week high.
8. RS rank in the top quartile (typically RS ≥ 70).

## Fresh-Pass Entry Rule

The stock satisfied 0 of 8 OR failed at least 2 of the conditions over the prior 20 trading days. On the trigger day, it satisfies all 8 simultaneously for the first time.

## Exit Rules

- **Stop Loss:** 1.5x ATR below entry, or below SMA50 — whichever is tighter.
- **Target 1:** 2.0x ATR above entry — scale 33%.
- **Target 2:** Trail with a swing-low or SMA50 stop.
- **Hard exit:** A close below SMA50 OR the template breaks (any single condition fails).

## Ideal Market Conditions

Bull market. The Trend Template by construction requires a stock in a long-running uptrend with a rising SMA200 — conditions that exist only in bull markets. In ranging or downtrending markets, fresh passes are sparse or absent (correctly).

## Source Attribution

Mark Minervini. *Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard* (2013) introduces the Trend Template; *Think and Trade Like a Champion* (2016) elaborates with examples. The "fresh pass" framing is a derived implementation — most practitioners screen the template daily as a static filter; gating on the FIRST satisfaction day in N days is the operational specialization.
