# Proximity Pullback (52-Week High)

A pullback continuation setup with a strong cross-sectional filter: only stocks within ~5% of their 52-week high qualify. The proximity filter restricts the universe to the leadership cohort, where pullback-and-resume patterns are statistically the most reliable.

## Why It Works

A pullback in a mid-pack stock is qualitatively different from a pullback in a leadership stock. The leadership cohort — stocks within 5% of their 52-week high — has demonstrated sustained demand over a 12-month window. A short-term dip in this group typically resolves upward because the structural buyers are still present.

The proximity filter does most of the work. Conventional pullback setups in stocks that are 30–40% off their highs have a much weaker base rate because the prior structure that produced the 52w high may already be broken.

## Entry Rules

1. Stock within 5% of its 52-week high BEFORE the pullback begins.
2. Pullback of 3–10% from the recent high over 3–10 bars.
3. Pullback occurs on declining volume (no distribution).
4. Price holds above the rising EMA21 or SMA50.
5. Bounce candle closes in the upper half of its range on volume above the 20-day average.

## Exit Rules

- **Stop Loss:** Below the pullback low or 1.5x ATR below entry.
- **Target 1:** Prior swing high (recent 52w-high level) — scale 50%.
- **Target 2:** 2.0x the pullback depth above entry.
- **Trailing Stop:** Swing-low stop or 1.5x ATR.

## Ideal Market Conditions

Bull market. The setup requires a meaningful cohort of stocks to be within 5% of their 52-week highs — which by construction does not happen in bearish or sharply ranging regimes. In a strong bull market, the proximity-pullback universe regenerates constantly as new leaders emerge.

## Source Attribution

The 52-week-high proximity filter has academic backing — Hong, Lim & Stein (2000) and George & Hwang (2004) both showed that proximity to 52-week high is a stronger momentum predictor than raw return momentum. The pullback-entry trigger is composite — variants appear across CAN SLIM, Minervini's school, and Driehaus's framework.

**Relevant paper:** George, T. J. & Hwang, C.-Y. (2004). *The 52-Week High and Momentum Investing.* Journal of Finance, 59(5), 2145–2176.
